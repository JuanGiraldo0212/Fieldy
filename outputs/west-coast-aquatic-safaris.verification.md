- **Meets minimum viable record:** no. The venue block is complete: id, name, address, latitude, longitude, category, date and a hero image with alt are all present. What is missing is **`age_basis` and an age or grade range on every program**. The site publishes no minimum age for any tour or for either vessel. It says only that people of all ages are welcome to tour aboard and that the tours are great for kids and adults of all ages, which is a welcome rather than a range, and it publishes no weight or height rule either. For a daycare group that is the decisive unknown, and it is left visibly empty rather than filled in. The record also carries no child price and no group rate, for the same reason.

- **Confidence:** medium to high on what is recorded, low on what a daycare director actually needs. - **Recommended follow up by phone or email** (250-725-9227, or info@whalesafaris.com):
  1. **Price** — what does a child pay, and is there a group or daycare rate? The only figure on the site is $159 per person and it is not visible anywhere a parent would look.
  2. **Youngest age** — is there any minimum age, on either boat? Nothing is published, and the two vessels are very different: a 12 passenger cruiser and a 37 passenger catamaran.
  3. **Capacity** — how many children can come on one booking, and would a group of ours get WASCO or NANUQ?
  4. **Lead time** — how far ahead does a group need to book, and does the 48 hour cancellation rule apply to a group the same way?
  5. **Which tour** — the bear trip stays in sheltered water and runs about two hours; the whale trip goes into open ocean for two and a half. Ask which they would put a group of under fives on.
  6. **Lunch space** — is there anywhere a group can eat before or after? They do not serve food and suggest eating afterwards.
  7. **Washrooms** — confirmed on every boat and at the office, but ask about a change table.
  8. **Bus parking** — nothing is published. The directions mention street parking on Fourth Street and a pay lot at Third and Main, neither of which sounds like somewhere to leave a bus.
  9. **Rain** — tours run rain or shine with rain jackets provided and a heated cabin, so ask what actually happens in high wind, since that is the crew's call and they say they will be in touch.
