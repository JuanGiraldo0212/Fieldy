Every program `source_url` was re-fetched cold on a trailing-slash variant to force a genuine second request, and each evidence quote was matched against the page text character for character after whitespace normalisation. The Overview and Camping pages were re-fetched to confirm the washroom and elevator lines.
- **Fields corrected:** 2
  - `venue.name`: "McLean Mill National Historic Park" (from the tracker) -> "McLean Mill National Historic Site". The site uses "National Historic Site" in its page titles, its meta description, its headings and its own history text. Only one footer line uses "Park", and the Google Maps link in the footer says "McLean Mill Historic Park". The tracker's wording is kept in the file name only.
  - `program.group-guided-tour.cost_per_group_cad`: 50 -> null, with `cost_per_child_cad` set to 5 instead. The $50 is a tour guide fee charged on top of per person rates, not a flat group price, so recording it as the group cost would have understated what a class of twenty pays. It is carried verbatim in `extra_fees_note`.
- **Fields set to null after review:** 4
  - `hosts_daycare_groups` — the site never mentions preschool or daycare groups and gives no minimum age, so there is no reason to say false and no statement to say true.
  - `wheelchair_accessible` — the only accessibility sentence on the site is the elevator in the mill. That says nothing about the roads, the dam crossing or the rest of the grounds, so the flag stays null and the sentence is kept verbatim in the facility notes.
  - `has_lunch_space` — the wedding pages describe a rented hall and courtyard, and a visitor review on the About page mentions a buffet. Neither is a statement that a visiting group has somewhere to eat.
  - `program.self-guided-site-visit.tax_included` — the line "(GST and Heritage Fee applicable on all tours)" sits inside the private tour rates block but says "all tours". Ambiguous, so it is recorded as false on the guided tour only and left null on the self-guided admission, with the ambiguity in gaps.
- **Season check (asked for specifically):** the site is **closed for the season right now** and says so five separate times on the home page. The heritage buildings, the administration office, the Mill Store, the gift shop and the washrooms are all shut, and self-guided tours are "not available at this time". What is still running is: the grounds, open dawn to dusk 365 days a year at your own risk, and guided tours for groups, which the home page marks "Open for Reservations Only" and says can be accommodated most days with at least seven days notice. The tours page says private tours are booking for the 2026 season. The train to the mill is not running this year. The site never publishes the dates its staffed season opens or closes, so `months_offered` is null on every program and that is flagged as unknown in gaps rather than guessed.

The venue field keeps 250-723-1376 because it is the number on the contact page and the footer as well as the tours page, and the note tells the director to try the other one.

  - `practical_summary` is generated from the washroom and elevator facts plus the washroom, lunch, rain backup, bus parking and capacity gaps.
- **Location:** `geo_source` is `site_embed`, not `geocode_pending`. No geocoding service was available in this run, but the venue's own home page publishes a "DIRECTIONS to Mclean Mill" Google Maps link whose destination pin is carried in the URL as `!1d-124.829171!2d49.3086674`, giving 49.30867, -124.82917. That is a coordinate published on the venue's own domain, which the spec ranks above geocoding, so it was used rather than left pending. It was re-confirmed on a second cold fetch of the home page. **If the orchestrator wants this run held to `geocode_pending` for consistency, these two values are the only thing to change.** The address itself, 5633 Smith Road, Port Alberni, BC, V9Y 8M1, is on the footer of every page.
- **Images:** three recorded, one of them a hero. All three sit on the site's own builder CDN (content.app-sources.com, the same account id as every other asset on the site), all are https and absolute, and all three full size URLs were confirmed present verbatim in the source of the page recorded in `found_on_url` and confirmed to return a JPEG. No captions were invented and no `rights_note` was set, because the only copyright line on the site is the sitewide footer. **Every photograph on this site has an empty alt attribute, and no browser was available in this run, so all three alts are `generated` from the image file names and the surrounding page text rather than from looking at the images.** They are deliberately minimal for that reason, and this is stated in gaps. The Open Graph image was skipped as the hero because its file name (`0-1-1205818.jpg`) says nothing about its content and it could not be viewed; the page banner photograph used instead appears as the header on the home, tours and about pages.
- **Retrieval:** the site is a JavaScript-era page builder but it serves complete server-rendered HTML, so plain fetching worked throughout. No RETRIEVAL NOTE is needed and no browser was required for text.
- **Meets minimum viable record:** no, by a narrow margin. The venue block is complete: `id`, `name`, `address`, `lat`, `lng`, `category`, `checked_on` and a `hero` image with `alt` are all present. What is missing is on the programs: the site publishes **no age or grade range for any tour**, so `age_basis`, `age_min_years`, `age_max_years`, `grade_min` and `grade_max` are null on all three. The age bands in the rate lists are prices, not a published age range, and converting them would be an invention. Every other program requirement is met: each has `id`, `name`, `comes_to_you`, a cost field or `is_free`, and `our_note`.
- **Confidence:** high on everything the site states. The prices, the 90 minute length, the seven days notice, the address, the washroom line and the closure wording are all unambiguous and were matched word for word on a second fetch. The record is thin on the things a daycare director most needs, but that thinness is the site's, not the extraction's.

  8. Bus parking — can a school bus turn and park at 5633 Smith Road?
